Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026

The short version

Nite Ride doesn't collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and the app doesn't connect to the internet.

What stays on your iPhone

Nite Ride remembers one setting so it can pick up where you left off: the last sound you chose. It's saved only on your iPhone, is never sent anywhere, and is removed if you delete the app.

Sounds

All sounds are generated on your iPhone as they play. Nite Ride doesn't stream audio or use your microphone.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you control Nite Ride with Siri or the Shortcuts app, Apple processes your request under Apple's Privacy Policy. Nite Ride only receives the action to perform, such as which sound to play or to pause.

CarPlay

Nite Ride shows its sound list and playback controls on your car's screen through CarPlay. It doesn't access your location or any data from your vehicle.

Children

Nite Ride is made for parents and caregivers, and it doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
