Nite Ride

The road to sleep.

Nite Ride plays white, pink, and brown noise on iPhone and right from your car's screen with CarPlay. Every sound is generated live on your phone, so it never loops and never ends.

Get help

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub. You'll need a free GitHub account. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and your car if it's a CarPlay problem.

Issues are public, so please don't include personal information.

Common questions

Nite Ride doesn't show up in CarPlay

On your iPhone, open Settings › General › CarPlay, choose your car, then tap Customize and make sure Nite Ride is included.

The sound stopped when I unplugged or turned off the car

That's on purpose. When CarPlay or headphones disconnect, Nite Ride pauses instead of suddenly playing from your phone's speaker. Tap play to start again.

How do I use Siri?

Say “Play brown noise in Nite Ride” (or white, or pink), “Start Nite Ride” to play your last sound, or “Pause Nite Ride.” You'll also find these actions in the Shortcuts app.

What's the difference between the sounds?

  • White noise: bright and even. Good at masking road hiss.
  • Pink noise: softer and balanced, like steady rain.
  • Brown noise: deep and low, like distant surf.

Does it keep playing with the screen locked?

Yes. Control it from the Lock Screen, Control Center, your steering wheel, or CarPlay's Now Playing screen.
